Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Heavy Engineers collects, why it is used, and the choices available to visitors.

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Information we collect

Heavy Engineers is a static learning website. We may receive information you voluntarily submit through the contact or course-notification forms, such as your name, email address, selected course, and message.

We may also receive limited technical information from hosting, analytics, or advertising services, such as browser type, device information, approximate location, pages visited, and aggregated usage events.

How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to questions, manage locally saved course-notification requests, improve educational content, understand site performance, and protect the website from abuse. We do not sell personal information.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

External services used for forms, code libraries, or linked resources may process limited technical information under their own privacy policies. Heavy Engineers does not currently operate a first-party analytics dashboard or advertising program on these pages.

Data retention and your choices

We retain form submissions only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which they were provided. You can request access, correction, or deletion of information by contacting hello@heavyengineers.com.

Third-party links

Lessons and resources may link to external websites. Heavy Engineers is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@heavyengineers.com.
