Privacy Policy

How Heavy Engineers handles information when you visit the website or contact us.

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy applies to https://heavyengineers.github.io/ (“website”). Heavy Engineers is the name used for this educational website. Privacy questions: ayushnandanwar003@gmail.com.

Information you provide

If you use the contact form, you may provide your name, email address and message. The form is submitted through FormSubmit, a third-party form-forwarding service, and is configured to forward submissions to the contact email shown on this website. FormSubmit may process form content and technical information under its own terms and privacy policy. Do not submit sensitive personal information.

The course-notification form currently saves your name, email, selected course and submission time in browser local storage. It does not transmit that entry to Heavy Engineers or an email delivery service. Clearing this website's browser storage may remove it.

Information stored in your browser

The website uses local storage for learning progress, completed practice questions, SQL query history, editor drafts and locally saved course-notification entries. This information stays in your browser and supports those features. Clearing site data may remove saved progress and drafts.

Hosting and third-party resources

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub and its infrastructure may process technical information such as IP address, browser/device details and request logs. Some pages load Google Fonts and JavaScript libraries from public content-delivery networks; those providers may receive technical request information. External links are governed by the destination website's own privacy practices.

Advertising and cookies

Heavy Engineers has submitted a Google AdSense site application and added site-verification and ads.txt information. Site verification does not mean Google has approved the site or that advertisements are active. We will display AdSense advertisements only if Google approves the site and the account is eligible to serve ads.

If advertising is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies to serve, measure, limit or personalise advertisements, detect invalid traffic and prevent fraud. The technologies used and choices available may depend on your location, consent settings and Google's policies. Where legally required, appropriate consent or privacy choices will be presented before non-essential advertising technologies are used.

For information about Google's data practices, visit How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can also review Google Ads Settings and manage cookies through your browser. Blocking cookies may affect third-party features.

Affiliate links

Some future articles or resource pages may contain affiliate links. If you follow one and make a qualifying purchase or take another qualifying action, Heavy Engineers may receive a commission or benefit. Such relationships will be disclosed near the relevant recommendation. Transactions are handled by the third-party provider under its own terms and privacy policy.

Use, sharing and retention

Contact information is used to respond to enquiries. Browser-stored information supports learning and practice features. We do not sell personal information. Service providers may process information to provide hosting, form forwarding or other services, and information may be disclosed where required by law. Contact-form messages may remain in the receiving email account and FormSubmit systems according to their retention practices. Browser data remains until cleared or removed by the browser.

Your choices, privacy requests and complaints

You may choose not to submit the contact form, clear local website data, or use available advertising and browser privacy settings. You can request access, correction or deletion of information you submitted through the contact form by emailing ayushnandanwar003@gmail.com with enough detail for us to locate the message. We will review requests in accordance with applicable law and the information available to us.

The same email address is the privacy and complaints contact for this website. Please describe the concern and include a way for us to respond. This contact designation is not a representation that Heavy Engineers is a registered company or that a statutory grievance-officer appointment has been made where one is legally required.

Contact-form messages are received by email and may also be processed by FormSubmit. We do not currently operate a separate customer database or a formal automated deletion schedule for these messages. Retention by FormSubmit is governed by its own policies and systems.

Children

This website provides general educational content for a broad audience. The course-interest feature stores only a selected course name in the visitor's browser and does not ask for a name or email. The contact form should not be used by children to submit personal information without appropriate parent or guardian involvement. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate action.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the website, service providers, advertising or applicable requirements change. Privacy questions: ayushnandanwar003@gmail.com.
