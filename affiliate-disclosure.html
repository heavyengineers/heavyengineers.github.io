Affiliate Disclosure

How Heavy Engineers handles affiliate links and other commercial relationships.

Last updated: November 17, 2026

What is an affiliate link?

Heavy Engineers may publish links to products, tools, books, courses, software, or services offered by third parties. Some of those links may be affiliate links. If you follow an affiliate link and make a qualifying purchase or take another qualifying action, Heavy Engineers may receive a commission or other benefit at no additional cost to you, unless the offer states otherwise.

How we disclose affiliate relationships

Where a page contains affiliate links, we will place a clear disclosure near the relevant recommendation or link, using a label such as “Affiliate link” or “Affiliate”. A general disclosure on this page does not replace a disclosure beside an individual recommendation where that is needed to make the relationship clear.

Editorial independence

Affiliate compensation does not guarantee a positive review or recommendation. We aim to explain relevant features, limitations, suitability, and material conditions. You should independently assess whether a product or service meets your needs.

Third-party transactions

Purchases, subscriptions, refunds, warranties, delivery, customer support, and payment processing are handled by the third-party provider under its own terms and privacy policy. Heavy Engineers is not the seller unless a page expressly says otherwise.
